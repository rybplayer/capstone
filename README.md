# Project Limitations
Given the theoretical nature of our project, we cannot meet all website rubric
guidelines and have been advised by TA that we can ignore sections that are not
relevant to our project. For our rationale behind not including certain sections
see below.

## Simplicity & Information Architecture
### Core Content
Core content (Problem, Method, and Results) cannot all be satisfied, namely the
"Methods" section because our project does not have a traditional methodology
like other projects and would likely be a lot of trial and error that is out
of the scope of our final project.

### Low Cognitive load
Short paragraphs does not make sense in our context large amounts of text are
required to communicate our findings.

## Effectiveness
### Evaluation is Explicit
This section asks us to explicitly state our: metrics, baselines, comparisons,
and ablations as relevant. However, in our project, there are no traditional
metrics used like accuracy and there are no baselines to compare it to as we
explored a novel problem. As such it does not make sense for us to have explicit
evaluations.

### Rigor
This section asks us to describe our dataset and other data related subjects.
Given that we do not have a dataset and it would not make sense for us to have
a dataset, we ignore this section.

### Shows iteration
Like previously mentioned, it wouldn't make sense for us to show our iterations
as there would be no clear progress as it is mostly exploration of ideas that
may not be related at all to our final output.

## Communication Quality
### Tone
Our project does have a very technical tone, as one would expect from a theoretical
project. This requires us to have a "wall of text" by nature as we seek to
explain our findings in detail.

## Technical & Trust Signals
### Demo
We do not have a demo by nature since it is purely theoretical.

### Sensitive Data
We do not handle any data, therefore we do not handle any sensitive data.
